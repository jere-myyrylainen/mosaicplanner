# Tile placement and editing

Pointer presses enter through `beginPointer` in [editor.js](../../editor.js). `movePointer` updates the click-to-draw preview and passes drag events through to `updateGesture`. `endPointer` handles drag release and cancellation. [geometry.js](../../geometry.js) provides tile geometry, stroke sampling, and `PlacementIndex`.

## Preserve these behaviors

- Keep drawing tools in the left toolbar. Show separate rectangle and ellipse buttons in the adjacent toolbar while Shape is active. Start the adjacent toolbar beside the button immediately above its parent and hide it when the active tool has no available options. Show freehand rotation and selected tiles' controls above the canvas. Expose clipboard buttons when selecting or placing a copy.
- Use `PlacementIndex` for board fit, overlap, and grout gap checks. Candidate placement in a stroke must account for both earlier tiles and tiles accepted during that stroke. Use the same placement rules for the preview and commit.
- Use `tileVertices` for polygon outlines in both rendering and geometry. Ovals and leaves use a convex sampled outline for fit, contact, and erasing. Reserve facing-edge endpoint alignment for tiles with straight sides.
- Use `PlacementIndex.snapPlacement` for tile-to-tile snapping. It projects the moving tile's center onto the contact outline determined by both tile shapes, their rotation, and the grout gap. Use committed pieces as snap targets and the current action's index to validate candidates.
- For combined snapping, intersect the contact outlines of two distinct placed tiles or of a placed tile and a grid or custom guide edge. Contacts with the same outward direction slide together. Opposing contacts can hold a tile between neighbors. When nearby tiles compete, offer exact alignment of the ends of two facing edges within a short lateral tolerance, with the full grout gap. Validate candidates against the board and other pieces, using the translated selection for group moves.
- Keep freehand rotation and tile dimensions in the stroke spacing calculation. Line and Shape place tiles along the sampled path. Fill uses the snapped seed and then steps by tile dimensions plus grout width, expanding through connected accepted placements.
- With a mouse, Line and Shape start on the first press, preview as the pointer moves, and commit on the second press on the same canvas. Escape clears the preview and keeps the drawing tool active. Touch and pen use a drag.
- When moving a selection, compute the anchor tile's center from the snap targets and apply the same offset to every selected tile. Validate against the board and unselected tiles before committing the group.
- Rotate a selection around the average of the selected pieces' positions. Apply the same angle to their positions and orientations so the pivot, spacing, and cut edges stay aligned. The angle field sets an absolute angle for one tile and applies a signed turn for multiple tiles. Check that each rotated tile fits on a surface and leaves the required gap to unselected tiles. Commit the full rotation as one action.
- Copy captures the selected pieces in the editor clipboard. Paste previews one translation of their outlines and snaps the tile nearest the group's center. Keep the copied rotations and internal spacing. Check each copy against the board and existing tiles with the current grout gap. Commit the entire group with new IDs as one action, then select the copies. Escape or Cancel discards the preview and leaves the selection available.
- Split draws a straight line across one placed tile. Require both endpoints at or beyond its boundary and reject a line that fully crosses multiple tiles. Clip each side to the same cut line so the resulting pieces touch and cover the original outline. Sample a circular outline when cutting it. Preview the two resulting shapes, then replace the original with two individually editable pieces in one commit.
- Near a straight tile edge, show the starting point and how many millimetres of that edge each fragment retains. Snap both endpoints near corners and the quarter, midpoint, and three-quarter marks on the cutting tile. Give a valid 90° cut priority within 10° of perpendicular to the starting edge, including when the tile is rotated. Snap its endpoint to the intersection of the perpendicular and the actual boundary. If the starting corner would make the perpendicular run along an edge, try the unsnapped start. Use the adjusted line for both preview and commit.
- On circles, ovals, and leaves, show four snap points at the ends of their local symmetry axes. Keep the points aligned with the tile's rotation. A drag from an axis point toward the center locks to the opposite point. On a circle, a near-radial drag from any point on the rim locks to that diameter.
- Tile gestures work on both canvases. Each piece must fit completely on one surface. Drawing, splitting, filling, and erasing use the starting canvas. Piece moves and paste use the canvas under the pointer and its zoom to resolve placement.
- Preserve rectangle selection from empty canvas and Shift-click selection. Tile erase handles both one tile and a swept drag. Keep previews temporary and clean them up on pointer cancellation or lost pointer capture.

## Check changes

- Test an allowed and blocked placement with grout and rotation. Try a freehand drag, a line or outline, and a fill next to existing tiles.
- Use both shape buttons to preview and draw their outlines. Check that moving with the mouse button released updates the preview, the second press commits one undoable action, and Escape discards the preview with the tool still active. Verify toolbar alignment on narrow and wide screens and check that selecting another tool hides the options.
- Check group movement with snapping enabled, movement blocked by another tile, and undo after a drag or erase.
- Rotate mixed tiles and touching cut fragments with the button, the R shortcut, and a signed angle. Verify that the pivot stays fixed, one blocked tile rejects the whole rotation, and undo and redo restore the entire group.
- Copy and paste a single tile and a mixed selection with rotated tiles. Verify preview, group snapping, rejection when any tile collides or crosses the board edge, cancellation, repeated paste, and undo and redo of the whole group. Test the toolbar, keyboard shortcuts, and touch placement.
- Split a rotated tile and a round tile at zero and nonzero grout widths. Check incomplete and multi-tile cuts, cancellation, a cut close to an edge, and undo and redo. Move, rotate, copy, erase, and split a resulting piece again.
- Verify both dimensions and the right-angle mark on a rotated tile. Try a corner-to-corner diagonal, each end fraction, and an endpoint mark that conflicts with a valid right-angle cut. Release just inside the target edge and verify that the preview and committed fragments agree. Check contrast on dark and light tile faces.
- Cut a horizontal and a vertical leaf along both symmetry axes, including a rotated leaf on the worktable. Check a circle cut at an angle between the axis marks and verify that both fragments have the same area.
- Run `node tests/tile-splitting.test.cjs` to check retained area, touching saved outlines, and cut snapping.
- Place and split a tile on the worktable. Move a fragment to the mosaic and back at different zoom levels, and check both counts. Verify that a drop between surfaces leaves the original piece in place and that hiding an occupied table preserves its pieces.
- Run `node tests/worktable.test.cjs` to check fit, snapping, and opening saved pieces on both surfaces.
- Check tile snapping against rectangles, rotated tiles, triangles, and circles with zero and nonzero grout. Exercise both sides of the contact line and make sure selected tiles cannot act as snap targets for their own move.
- Check rotated diamonds, ovals, and leaves against tile edges and guides. Erase across a tapered tip and verify that a stroke through the empty corner of the tile's bounding box leaves the tile in place.
- Place and move a tile in a corner formed by two neighboring tiles. Verify the grout gap to each neighbor, fallback to a single target outside the combined range, and rejection when another tile blocks the corner.
- Run `node tests/tile-snapping.test.cjs` to verify alignment directly under either tile in a straight row, sliding between aligned positions, and genuine corners.
